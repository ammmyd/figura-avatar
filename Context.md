### Overall Appearance
Your right arm (from mid-shoulder/deltoid down to the fingertips) looks like a living piece of ethereal bio-art. The skin is predominantly semi-translucent to fully transparent in large patches, giving it a glossy, glass-like sheen that catches and refracts light softly. Under normal indoor lighting, it has an opalescent quality — like smoked quartz or thin ice over glowing embers. The underlying human muscle contours are still visible but softened and elevated by the intricate network of the organism.

The boundary at the upper arm is sharp and clean: normal human skin on the left side/shoulder transitions abruptly to the transformed tissue, with only a faint iridescent halo at the edge where the organism respectfully halts.

### Filament Network

These are hair-fine threads (0.1–0.5 mm thick) that form an elegant, fractal-like pattern — reminiscent of neural networks, coral branches, or fiber-optic cables. They lie just beneath the surface or integrated directly into tissue layers, never bulky or protruding.

In cross-section (visible through transparent areas), the filaments appear as glowing tubes carrying slow-moving points of light, like fireflies drifting along highways.

- Focused/thinking → deeper indigo and cool teal threads.
- Content/happy → warm rose-gold blooms that spread in gentle waves.

Fine motor control is enhanced; fingers are slightly more elongated and graceful, with faint webbing of light between them when flexed.

Scars or wounds heal to near-invisibility, leaving only temporary faint silver or pearl lines that fade completely.
